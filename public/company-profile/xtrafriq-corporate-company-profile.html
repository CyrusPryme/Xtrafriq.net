01 · Overview

Who we are

Xtrafriq Tech Consult is a Product Management & Technology Consulting Company. We help organisations turn operational needs into digital products and technology systems that can be run, governed and grown.

Our commercial identity is Product Management-Led Technology Delivery. Product strategy and project leadership sit at the centre of every engagement. Engineering, security, cloud and team enablement are how that strategy becomes a working system.

We are based in Accra, Ghana, and deliver remotely worldwide. Clients include public-sector and regulated operators, development partners, enterprises and growth organisations. The work we publish is shipped delivery—nationwide platforms, programme systems and production applications—not concept decks.

02 · Positioning

How we think about delivery

Mission

To help African organisations and startups transform ideas into production-ready digital products by combining product-led strategy, engineering excellence and local capacity building.

Vision

To be a trusted partner for technology transformation—delivering scalable, secure and inclusive digital systems that organisations can operate with confidence.

Product Management-Led Technology Delivery

Many technology engagements start with a feature list or a vendor stack. We start with the operating problem: who must use the system, what decisions it must support, and what “done” means in production. Roadmaps, OKRs and delivery cadence then keep engineering aligned to that outcome.

This is why product and project management are not add-on services. They are the method. Strategy without delivery leaves organisations with documents. Delivery without product leadership leaves organisations with software they cannot run or grow.

Product portfolio

We identify gaps, validate quickly and build scalable solutions of our own. Shipping products keeps the company sharp on user experience, engineering, reliability and growth—experience we bring into client work.

Client services

Product strategy, delivery leadership, engineering and enablement, designed to help teams ship with quality and to leave behind systems that operations can own.

These two engines share one standard: execution rigour. Approaches are proven in real products, then applied to client programmes with the same discipline around scope, quality gates and handover.

03 · Services continued

Security, cloud and enablement

Cybersecurity & Risk Management

Threat modelling, secure SDLC, access control and data-protection recommendations. The intent is to introduce the right controls early so organisations can ship without accumulating hidden risk that becomes expensive later.

Cloud Architecture & Reliability

Cloud foundations that are scalable, resilient and cost-aware. Work includes architecture review, scaling strategy, observability, deployment hygiene and reliability practices so products stay available as traffic and features grow.

Agile Delivery & Team Enablement

Sprint rituals, backlog hygiene, definition of done, QA gates, release planning and GitHub workflows. The outcome is a team that ships faster without trading away quality or predictability.

Typical project types

Operations and logistics programmes · education and training systems · digital transformation and tool rollout · marketing and growth initiatives · construction and infrastructure support workflows where technology improves coordination.

How an engagement typically runs

  • Discovery with operational and executive stakeholders—problem, constraints, success measures.
  • A delivery plan with scope, milestones, risks and governance that a counterpart can approve.
  • Iterative build or programme execution with QA gates and visible progress reporting.
  • Handover: documentation, operating routines and enablement so the organisation can run what was built.

06 · Approach

Why organisations instruct us

Production, not prototypes

Case work is operational: booking networks, consignment, freight, marketplaces and programme systems. We specialise in translating real-world operating needs into scalable digital solutions, including in regulated environments.

One method across strategy and build

Product leadership, project management and engineering are offered as a coherent delivery system. Counterparties deal with a consulting company that can own the outcome, not a loosely coupled set of contractors.

Accra base, worldwide delivery

Presence in Ghana with remote delivery for international counterparts. English-language engagement, reporting and documentation. Hybrid working where on-site workshops and stakeholder sessions are required.

Capacity, not only code

Through programme work such as the GIZ digital skills engagement, and through training 200+ African entrepreneurs in digital growth strategies, we treat enablement as part of delivery. Systems last when teams can run them.

Quality gates

Structured sprint planning, definition of done and QA gates are standard. Security and cloud concerns are introduced in delivery rather than bolted on after launch.

Community

A portion of proceeds is donated to Blacksheep Foundation and Awisi Foundation in Ghana, supporting youth development, community programmes and education. Commercial work remains the core of this profile; giving is noted as a company practice, not a substitute for delivery evidence.
